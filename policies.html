Community Rules & Policies

Guidance for using the SmartTech Appliances web app.

Community rules

  • Keep product reviews honest, relevant, and based on your experience.
  • Do not post abusive, unlawful, misleading, or promotional content in reviews or support messages.
  • Do not share passwords, payment details, or other sensitive personal information in public reviews.
  • Use your account only for your own purchases and contact support if you notice activity you do not recognize.

Shopping, returns & refunds

Order status and delivery updates are shown in your account. Return and refund requests may be submitted within 7 days after delivery and are reviewed by the store team. Approval records the decision; return shipping and any payment refund must be arranged separately because this web app does not connect to a payment refund provider.

Account & privacy

Your account details and saved addresses are used to support account access, order processing, and delivery. Use the account settings to update your profile or request account deletion. A deletion request is reviewed by the store team and does not immediately erase the account.

Using this web app

Please provide accurate account and order information. Product availability, prices, and order status are subject to confirmation by the store. Contact the team through the About Us page for questions.
